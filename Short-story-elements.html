Short Story Elements

Character

  • Characters are the people, animals, or beings in a story who drive the plot through their actions and choices.
  • Major Types:
  • Protagonist – The main character; central to the plot.
  • Antagonist – The character or force that opposes the protagonist.
  • Nemesis – A long-standing rival or enemy of the protagonist.
  • Character Types:
  • Round Characters – Well-developed, complex, and realistic.
  • Flat Characters – Simple and one-dimensional; not fully developed.
  • Dynamic Characters – Change or grow throughout the story.
  • Static Characters – Remain the same from beginning to end.

Symbolism

  • Symbolism is when a person, object, place, or event represents a deeper meaning beyond its literal sense.
  • Conventional: Symbols recognized across culture (e.g., a heart for love).
  • Contextual: Unique symbols developed by the author for the story.

Irony

  • Verbal Irony: Saying one thing but meaning the opposite.
  • Situational Irony: A difference between expected and actual outcomes.
  • Dramatic Irony: When the audience knows something the character does not.

Flashback

  • A flashback interrupts the present timeline to reveal events from the past.
  • *Note: A simple reference to the past is not a flashback.

Flash-forward

  • The story jumps ahead in time to reveal future events.
  • Advantages: Builds suspense, provides foresight.
  • Disadvantages: Risk of spoiling plot twists.

Pathetic Fallacy

  • When nature or objects reflect the emotions/mood of the story (e.g., storm during sadness).

Diction and Tone

  • Diction: The vocabulary style used (formal/informal).
  • Tone: The writer's attitude (e.g., angry, joyful).

Narrative POV

  • First Person, Second Person, and Third Person perspectives.

Reliable Narrator

  • Reliable Narrator: Aligns with the norms of the story.
  • Unreliable Types:
  • Naive – Lacks experience. 🙃
  • Outsider – Detached from other characters. 👤
  • Picaro – Embellishes/exaggerates. 🦹
  • Insane – Lost touch with reality. 🤪
  • Liar – Intentionally deceives. 🤥
